Add an event

A single historical event — a battle, a treaty, a coronation, a published work. The form generates a JSON file you can submit three ways.

What makes a good event entry

  • Specific. "Battle of Panipat (1526)" not "Mughal conquest of north India".
  • Verifiable. A date, a place, named participants. If you can't pin all three, it's probably not an event — it's a process.
  • Sourced twice. Cross-check against at least two independent sources before marking verified.
  • Well-pinned. The pin should be at a real place: the actual battlefield, the building where the treaty was signed, the city where the conference was held.
